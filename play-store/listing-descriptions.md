## Full description (4000 chars max)
StoreClear is a secure storage manager for Android. Find where your space went, remove clutter safely, and wipe sensitive files with multi-pass shredding—all on your device.

**See what is using your storage**
Get a clear breakdown of used space across media, apps, documents, downloads, and more. The dashboard shows total capacity at a glance so you know when it is time to clean up.

**Find duplicate files**
Scan folders for identical copies and recover space in one tap. Review grouped matches, expand details, and select duplicates to remove while keeping the original.

**Explore with a storage heatmap**
Navigate folders visually and spot the largest space hogs quickly. Drill into subfolders with breadcrumbs and a color-coded treemap-style view.

**Secure file shredder**
Permanently delete sensitive files using configurable overwrite passes based on DoD 5220.22-M style wiping. Queue jobs, track progress, and review destruction logs.

**More cleanup tools**
• Empty folder cleaner — remove leftover directories
• Cache cleaner — review app cache sizes and clear selected items
• Configurable hash algorithms (SHA-256 or fast MD5) for duplicate scans
• Adjustable shred intensity and scan depth in Settings

**Privacy first**
StoreClear is built for local operation. No accounts, no third-party analytics, and no uploading of your files. Scanning, hashing, and shredding run on your device.

Grant storage permissions when prompted so StoreClear can analyze and manage the folders you choose.

Find hidden space. Leave no trace.
